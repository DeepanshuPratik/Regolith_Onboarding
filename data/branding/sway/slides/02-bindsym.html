The bindings you'll reach for daily

sway ships with sensible defaults. These are the eight that earn their place — the ones a regular user presses without thinking — taken straight from /etc/sway/config.

  • $mod + ReturnOpen a terminal. The one binding everyone writes; sway's default is alacritty.
  • $mod + DApp launcher. wofi by default; type, hit Enter.
  • $mod + QClose this window. Focused window only; sway won't ask twice.
  • $mod + FFullscreen. A single window across the output.
  • $mod + VSplit vertically. Stack a new window beside the focused one.
  • $mod + HSplit horizontally. Above/below instead of left/right.
  • $mod + Shift + QReload the config. No logout, no warning.
  • $mod + Shift + CReload, plus the bar. When you change bar too.
