Workspaces by name, not number

i3 and sway both let you give each workspace a name. The number stays for switching; the name is what the bar shows and what your muscle memory reaches for. The defaults are 1…10; renaming them takes one line each.

  • 1: termBrowsers and editors. The work surface.
  • 2: webBrowsers and reference. Switch to it to look something up, switch back when done.
  • 3: chatA persistent chat window. Visible from anywhere; you set it sticky.
  • 9: scratchA scratchpad. $mod+minus hides it; $mod+Shift+minus shows it again.
  • $mod + 1…9Switch to that workspace. By number, by name — whichever you're looking at.
  • $mod + Shift + 1…9Send the focused window there. Move a window without focusing it first.

Names are the config's job, not the bar's — change them in ~/.config/sway/config under workspace.
