One laptop, one dock, one setting

Wayland's per-output scaling is the single feature that earns the switch. Plug a 4K display into a HiDPI laptop and the panel can stay 1× while the monitor runs at 2×, or vice versa — without the blurred text you remember from X. Regolith wires this into the bar and the workspace switcher, so the result is consistent across apps.

  • Settings → DisplaysScale per output. Drag the slider while the panel is open; live and reversible.
  • Super + ,Reload panel geometry. Useful when an external monitor wakes up smaller than expected.
  • gnome-randrFrom a terminal. When the GUI can't see the monitor, the command-line can.

Fractional scaling (1.25, 1.5) is supported; whole-number is sharper, especially on small text.
