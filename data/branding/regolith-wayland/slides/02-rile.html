Rile is the control centre

GNOME users expect a quick settings panel. Regolith ships one: Rile, the GTK control panel that talks to sway over sway-ipc. Open it and you can pick a wallpaper, swap the bar style, change the keyboard layout or reset any of it back to defaults without editing a config file.

  • Super + Ctrl + SpaceOpen Rile. The settings shortcut — it remembers its last tab.
  • Look & feelWallpaper, bar, gaps. Live preview; close the panel and the change is written to the config.
  • ShortcutsBindings without a text editor. Per-app and global — a remap is a checkbox.
  • ResetBack to Regolith defaults. If your tweaks start fighting, this is the escape hatch.
