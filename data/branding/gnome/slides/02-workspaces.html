Workspaces that follow your work

GNOME only shows you the workspaces you're using. Open enough windows and a new desktop appears on the right; close them all and it goes away. The desk reshapes itself, so you don't have to plan it.

  • Super + Page Up/DownMove between desktops. Or flick up/down on a touchpad with three fingers.
  • Super + Shift + Page Up/DownSend this window to a neighbour. Tidy up while you stay focused.
  • Super + drag a windowPlace it on any desktop. The cursor shows you where it will land before you let go.
  • Touchpad: pinchThe same, with fingers. Four-finger pinch in for overview; pinch out to dismiss.
