Everything is one keystroke away

GNOME's overview is a search box, a window switcher, and a workspace switcher rolled into one screen. Reach it once, decide once.

  • SuperActivities. The one key that opens every door on this desktop.
  • Type a nameOpen an app or file. No launcher to remember — just start typing.
  • Alt + F2A command, without leaving the keyboard. Useful for the rare thing the launcher can't find.
  • Super + LLock the screen. Faster than the menu and works from any focused window.

Super is the key with the Windows or Command logo.
