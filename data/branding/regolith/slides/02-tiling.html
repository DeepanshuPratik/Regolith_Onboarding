Ten desktops, one keystroke apart

Instead of stacking every window on one screen, spread your work across numbered workspaces — mail on one, editor on another, browser on a third — and move between them instantly.

  • Super + 1…9Jump to a workspace. Direct, no cycling.
  • Super + TabNext workspace. When you do not care about the number.
  • Super + Shift + 1…9Send this window there. Tidy up without a mouse.
  • Super + ← ↑ ↓ →Move focus. By direction. h j k l work too.
