Learn them by using them

Reading a shortcut list does not build muscle memory. The workflows on the next screen prompt you for one shortcut at a time and wait until you press it — and the real action happens, so you see what it does.

  • PlayStarts a workflow. This window shrinks aside so you can see your desktop.
  • EscapeStops at any point. Nothing is left changed behind you.

Take them in any order. Launching Applications is a good first one.
