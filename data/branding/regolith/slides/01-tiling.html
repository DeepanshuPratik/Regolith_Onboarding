Your windows arrange themselves

Regolith tiles every window automatically. Nothing overlaps, nothing needs dragging, and nothing gets lost behind something else. Open a second window and the space simply divides.

  • Super + EnterA terminal. Opens beside whatever is already there.
  • Super + SpaceAnything else. Start typing an app name and press Enter.
  • Super + Shift + ?Every shortcut. The one to remember if you forget the rest.

Super is the key with the Windows or Command logo.
